Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Glowmelan is run by Asad Arain, based in Pakistan, who is the "controller" of your data. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your skin answers and photos stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your skin quiz answers, routine, streak and reminder times (Plus users can choose to back these up — see below).
  • Your progress photos. They are made smaller and saved only in your browser's storage on this device. We never upload them.
  • Backup files you choose to save. You control where they go.

If you use the free plan, none of this leaves your phone. If you have Plus and are signed in, the items above (but never photos) are also saved in your account so you can use them on another phone.

What leaves your phone

  • Cloud backup (Plus only, off until you turn it on): if you have Plus, are signed in and choose Turn on cloud backup, your quiz answers, routine, streak history, reminder times and tracked-spot position are saved to your account so they work on any phone. Quiz answers can include health information (for example pregnancy, melasma, raised scars or prescription use), so we only save them with your explicit consent. You can turn it off in Learn → Account, which deletes the cloud copy. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Login emails: your sign-in code is sent through Resend, our email provider.
  • Hosting: the app is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you open it.
  • Account (only if you sign in): your email and Plus status are stored with Supabase, our database provider, so Plus stays yours on any phone. Signing in by itself does not upload your quiz answers, routine or photos.
  • Payments: if you subscribe to Glowmelan Plus, payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. They collect your email and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive your email and subscription status, never your full card number.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use cookies or advertising trackers. The app saves your data in your browser's own storage only so the app can work.
  • To see how the app is used and catch errors, we count simple anonymous events (for example "quiz finished" or "photo taken") with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies, does not track you across sites, and never receives your quiz answers, photos, email or other personal details.

Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • Account, Plus and payments: to provide the service you signed up for (contract).
  • Cloud backup of skin and health answers: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning cloud backup off.
  • Security, fixing problems and anonymous usage counts: our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe, working and improving.

Where your data is stored

Our providers (Supabase, Resend, GitHub and Lemon Squeezy) store data on servers in the United States and other countries. When data leaves the UK or EU, these providers use the safeguards the law requires, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Cloud backup: until you turn it off, delete your data, or ask us to delete your account.
  • Account and Plus status: while you have an account. We delete it within 30 days of your request.
  • Payment records: kept by Lemon Squeezy for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state laws such as California's and Washington's), you can ask us to: see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, give you a copy, or stop using it. You can also withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Photos and face data

We do not create face scans, face templates or any biometric identifiers. Photo checks (like "too dark") run on your phone and only look at brightness.

Children

Glowmelan is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Open Learn → Your data → Delete all data. This removes everything on your phone and, if you are signed in, your cloud backup too. To delete your account itself (email and Plus status) or payment records, email us and we will delete them within 30 days, and ask Lemon Squeezy to do the same where the law allows.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show a note in the app.
