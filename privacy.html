Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Glowmelan is a free app run by Asad Arain, based in Pakistan, who is the "controller" of your data. In short: your skin answers and photos stay on your phone. We have no accounts and no server that stores them.

What stays on your phone

  • Your skin quiz answers (which can include health information such as pregnancy or skin conditions), routine, streak and reminder times.
  • Your progress photos and dark spot tracker. Photos are made smaller and saved only in your browser's storage on this device. We never upload them.
  • Backup files you choose to save. You control where they go.

We cannot see any of this. If you clear your browser data or lose your phone without a backup file, we cannot recover it.

What leaves your phone

  • Hosting: the app is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you open it.
  • Anonymous usage counts: to see how the app is used and catch errors, we count simple events (for example "quiz finished" or "photo taken") with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies, does not track you across sites, and never receives your quiz answers, photos or other personal details.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use cookies. The app saves your data in your browser's own storage only so the app can work.

Why we use data (legal basis)

Hosting logs and anonymous usage counts are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe, working and improving. Everything else stays on your device and is not processed by us.

Where data is stored

GitHub and GoatCounter may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, using the safeguards the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state laws), you have rights over your personal data, including to ask what we hold, to delete it and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Because we do not store your skin data, you can delete it yourself at any time — see below. For any question, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Photos and face data

We do not create face scans, face templates or any biometric identifiers. Photo checks (like "too dark") run on your phone and only look at brightness.

Children

Glowmelan is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Open Learn → Your data → Delete all data, or clear this site's data in your browser. This removes everything Glowmelan stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if we add advertising — we will update this page and the date above, and show a note in the app before the change applies.
